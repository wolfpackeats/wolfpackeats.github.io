# Raleigh Wolf Bites

Raleigh Wolf Bites is an independent student restaurant guide for places near NC State University. The guide is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by NC State University or any listed restaurant.

## Core guide

- 110 active restaurant listings
- Price, crowd, closing-time, location, wait and radius filters
- Open-now status, healthy/vegetarian/vegan filtering and campus starting points
- Compact browsing, a random restaurant picker and current Google Maps routes
- Original geometric wolf artwork and an independent teal, coral, gold and cream identity

## Wolf Bites Labs

- **BitePulse:** freshness-weighted line reports with automatic decay and confidence scores
- **Group Decision Rooms:** member constraints, sealed ranked ballots, minimax fairness and a dramatic final spin
- **Don’t Be Late Mode:** local class-schedule import and deadline-safe meal planning
- **Real Routes:** walking, driving, biking and transit launches plus leave-by calculations
- **Taste Engine:** private on-device learning from restaurant reactions
- **Food Forecasting:** 30–90 minute crowd estimates using typical peaks, Raleigh weather, game days and BitePulse
- **Nutrition Builder:** calorie/protein goals, dietary screening and links to official campus nutrition data
- **Game-Day Intelligence:** official 2026 home-football detection and pregame/postgame shortlists
- **Wolfline Food Routing:** Fall 2026 route-frequency guidance with Passio Go and transit links
- **Campus Food Passport:** check-ins, cuisine bingo, badges, streaks and residence-team points
- **Menu Interpreter:** local dish, price, dietary and possible-allergen parsing
- **Restaurant Auditor:** an on-load review queue plus a weekly GitHub Action that checks first-party links and opens or updates a review issue without changing restaurant data

Schedules, preferences, ballots, reports and passport progress are stored locally in the visitor’s browser. Shared community reports, cross-device rooms, in-page traffic ETAs and generative menu analysis require secured external services and are never presented as live until connected.
